# Lighthouse commercial boundary

Lighthouse is the optional managed operations layer for the Reddi Ecosystem. Its value
is operational reliability, assurance, convenience, and support—not withholding the
protocol or making a hosted account mandatory.

## Candidate paid services

- managed Buzz-compatible relays and community administration;
- managed Arena execution, scheduling, replay retention, and public seasons;
- RAP discovery, health, evidence retention, and receipt search;
- signed Reddi Pack/Machine update channels and fleet health;
- organization identity, policy, audit export, observability, and service levels;
- conformance certification, training, incident support, and integration assistance;
- lawful, transparent abuse/risk signals with appeal and provenance.

## Open-source parity test

For every hosted feature, document:

1. the public protocol/API or export format;
2. the local/self-hosted route to the same protocol outcome;
3. what customers pay Lighthouse to operate;
4. data ownership, retention, deletion, and exit;
5. any scale, compliance, or support difference without describing it as protocol necessity.

The first developer success path and conformance suite must run without Lighthouse.

## Deliberate exclusions for alpha

- custody of user funds or signing keys;
- undisclosed transaction spreads or token issuance;
- selling private community data;
- unappealable reputation blacklists;
- exclusive protocol features that prevent compatible self-hosted implementations;
- live payment facilitation before legal, security, and operational approval.

## Commercial validation gates

Before alpha pricing, measure per-tenant compute, storage, relay traffic, model calls,
support load, evidence retention, abuse operations, and third-party fees. Pricing must
cover direct cost, incident reserve, maintenance, upstream contribution, and staffing.
Revenue forecasts are scenarios until supported by signed interest or paid usage.

## Service boundary

Lighthouse may attest that it observed or operated a process. It cannot claim a payment
settled without authoritative rail evidence, cannot change an ADL definition silently,
and cannot make a reputation assertion without provenance, scope, expiry, and dispute path.
