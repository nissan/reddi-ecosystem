## Executive finding

The programme has substantial implementation evidence, but the available public record
does **not** yet prove completion of the May–July 2026 grant plan or the August success
targets. Code, tests, issues, and devnet deployments are not substitutes for package
publication, external adoption, workshop attendance, eligible users, transaction volume,
or approved mainnet evidence.

The truthful programme posture is therefore **recovery**, not retrospective completion.
No live transaction or synthetic activity is authorized by this audit. The revised sequencing makes Solana rails with AUDD payments the first concrete implementation and acceptance-evidence path for Solana Superteam Australia and AUDD obligations; MPP, AP2, x402, ACP/UCP, Stripe-style rails, and other standards remain comparison fixtures or later adapters until that evidence pack is reviewed.

## Evidence method

Each claim is classified as:

- `verified`: reproducible primary evidence satisfies the stated measure;
- `partial`: some required evidence exists but the measure is not satisfied;
- `planned`: an issue or document exists without completion evidence;
- `blocked`: a prerequisite or human decision prevents lawful/safe completion;
- `superseded`: a later approved commitment replaced it;
- `unknown`: the available public and workspace evidence cannot decide it.

Evidence must identify the environment, date range, source system, calculation rule,
and exclusions. Self-authored prose alone is not proof of an external outcome.

## Decision gates

- Only a human maintainer can classify a contractual commitment as superseded.
- Only authorized operators can approve live/mainnet evidence runs.
- Legal/privacy review is required before publishing user, payment, or partner evidence.
- Grantor communication must distinguish delivery facts from remediation forecasts.
- MPP/AP2/x402 or other public no-spend standards demonstrations cannot be presented as satisfying Solana Superteam Australia or AUDD acceptance evidence unless the governing text and acceptance authority explicitly approve that substitution.
