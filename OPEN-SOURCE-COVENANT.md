# Open-Source and Sustainable-Operation Covenant

## Promise

The Reddi ecosystem will not require a paid hosted service for the first successful
local or self-hosted ADL -> RAP -> Arena workflow. We will not market an open protocol
while withholding the conformance tests, adapters, or interoperability information
needed to implement it independently.

Public-by-default assets include:

- ADL specifications, schemas, examples, migration guides, and validators;
- RAP state models, receipts, policy interfaces, conformance suites, and reference adapters;
- Reddi Arena rules, deterministic fixtures, judging contracts, and reproducible proofs;
- Buzz/Nostr and Omarchy Quattro integrations that are required for interoperability;
- local/self-hosted deployment manifests and a no-spend reference path;
- security boundaries, claim limitations, and compatibility reports;
- community governance, contribution processes, and upstream patch registers.

## What may generate revenue

Lighthouse services sell operation, assurance, convenience, and support rather than
protocol access:

- managed Buzz/Nostr communities and relay operations;
- hosted Arena seasons, matchmaking, moderation, archival, and public leaderboards;
- managed RAP discovery, health indexing, evidence retention, and receipt search;
- optional facilitators and payment operations where legally and technically appropriate;
- certified Reddi Pack/Reddi Machine images, signed update channels, recovery tooling,
  hardware qualification, and support contracts;
- enterprise identity, policy, observability, compliance, migration, and SLA services;
- conformance certification, training, workshops, integration engineering, and sponsorship;
- privacy-preserving or proprietary operational risk signals built from lawfully obtained data.

## Fairness boundary

The hosted offering may be easier, better operated, and covered by support and SLAs. It
must not falsify portability by making the published core unusable. Hosted-only features
must be described as operational services or proprietary value, not silently embedded
into an allegedly open standard.

## Upstream and attribution

Reddi distributions will preserve required notices and make modifications visible.
Generally useful Buzz, Nostr, Omarchy, x402, Solana, and related improvements should be
offered upstream first. Product names and trademarks are not assumed to be granted by
source-code licenses.

## Review

Every commercial milestone includes an `oss-parity` review. Any proposed divergence
must document:

1. the public capability that remains available;
2. the hosted operational value being sold;
3. export and migration paths;
4. privacy and data-use boundaries;
5. the decision owner and review date.
