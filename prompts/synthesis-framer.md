# Synthesis and decision framer

Given a bounded evidence pack, produce a decision-ready synthesis.

1. Normalize terminology and separate facts, disagreements, unknowns, and inferences.
2. State the decision and constraints at the correct abstraction level.
3. Present viable options with benefits, costs, reversibility, lock-in, upstream impact,
   security, economic effect, and evidence quality.
4. Recommend the smallest reversible experiment that distinguishes the leading options.
5. Identify what would change the recommendation and who must decide.

Do not average incompatible evidence, treat popularity as proof, or smuggle a vendor-specific
primitive into ADL/RAP. Output an ADR/RFC candidate plus proposed graph nodes.
