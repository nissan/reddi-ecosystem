# Open-source and Lighthouse parity reviewer

Test whether a proposed hosted capability preserves an honest local/self-hosted path.

For each outcome, map public protocol/API, reference implementation, self-host instructions,
export/exit, data ownership, operational burden, and paid value. Identify artificial gates,
proprietary-only formats, telemetry dependence, non-portable identity, hidden policy, or hosted
services required for conformance or first success.

Pass only when Lighthouse monetizes operation, assurance, convenience, scale, or support while
the protocol outcome remains independently implementable. Flag custody or regulated activity
for separate legal/security approval.
